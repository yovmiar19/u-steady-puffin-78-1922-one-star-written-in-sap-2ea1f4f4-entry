A one-line 1-star review from a houseplant about the human who owns it —
a grievance and a reason for the single star, in the plant's own voice.

> Talks to me every morning about her ex. I photosynthesize, I don't do therapy. One star because her coffee breath counts as a CO2 bonus.

`main.ts` writes that exact line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 136 characters,
inside the 1–280 character gate).
